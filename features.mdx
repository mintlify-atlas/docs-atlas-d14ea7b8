## Core Features

### Centralized Subscription Management

Track, edit, and organize all your subscriptions in one unified dashboard.

**Key Capabilities:**
- Add unlimited subscriptions (no restrictions)
- Edit subscription details anytime
- Delete subscriptions when cancelled
- Search and filter by service name
- View all subscriptions in a list or calendar format
- Support for both monthly and yearly billing cycles

**Supported Subscription Types:**
- Streaming services (Netflix, Spotify, Disney+, etc.)
- SaaS tools (GitHub, Notion, Figma, etc.)
- Cloud storage (Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud, etc.)
- Gaming subscriptions (Xbox Game Pass, PlayStation Plus, etc.)
- News and media (New York Times, Medium, etc.)
- Any custom recurring expense

<Tip>
  Subflow includes over 100 pre-configured services with icons from the [SVGL](https://svgl.app) library. If your service isn't listed, you can easily create a custom one.
</Tip>

### Calendar-Based Tracking

Visualize all your subscription payment dates on an intuitive monthly calendar.

**How It Works:**

The calendar displays:
- Each day of the month in a grid layout
- Subscriptions on their billing dates
- Service icons for quick recognition
- Payment amounts in your preferred currency
- Multiple subscriptions per day (stacked or expandable)

**Navigation:**
- Click the left/right arrows to switch months
- View past payments and future charges
- Today's date is highlighted
- Days with subscriptions are clearly marked

**Interaction:**
- **Desktop**: Hover over subscriptions to see a quick preview, click to edit or delete
- **Mobile**: Tap subscriptions to open a detailed drawer with actions

<Note>
  Yearly subscriptions are displayed on their payment date, but by default they're amortized (divided by 12) and shown on the calendar every month. You can change this behavior in Preferences.
</Note>

### Smart Add with AI Detection

Quickly add subscriptions using AI-powered content analysis.

**How Smart Add Works:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Input Method">
    Select either:
    - **Upload**: Receipt, screenshot, or invoice image (up to 10MB)
    - **Text**: Paste email receipt, subscription details, or description
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="AI Analysis">
    Subflow's AI (powered by Google Gemini) analyzes the content and extracts:
    - Service name
    - Subscription price
    - Currency
    - Billing date
    - Payment cycle (monthly/yearly)
    - Matches against known services in the SVGL library
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review and Edit">
    The AI-detected information is displayed in an editable form. You can:
    - Correct any mistakes
    - Adjust the service selection
    - Modify the price or date
    - Re-analyze if needed
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Save Subscription">
    Click **Add Subscription** to save it to your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What Content Works Best:**
- Email receipts with pricing details
- Screenshots of subscription confirmation pages
- Invoice PDFs or images
- Text descriptions like "Netflix Premium - $15.99/month starting on the 1st"

**Supported File Formats:**
- Images: JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, HEIF
- Text: Plain text pasted directly
- Maximum file size: 10MB

<Warning>
  Smart Add requires clear, readable content. Blurry images or incomplete text may result in inaccurate detection. You can always use Manual Add for full control.
</Warning>

### Multi-Currency Support

Track subscriptions in any currency with automatic real-time conversion.

**Currency Features:**

- **150+ Supported Currencies**: From USD to Bitcoin, including crypto
- **Real-Time Exchange Rates**: Updated regularly from reliable financial APIs
- **Per-Subscription Currency**: Each subscription can have its own currency
- **Display Currency**: View all amounts in your preferred currency
- **Automatic Conversion**: All calculations done automatically

**How It Works:**

1. Each subscription stores its price in the original currency
2. When viewing the calendar or analytics, select your preferred display currency
3. Subflow converts all amounts using current exchange rates
4. Monthly spend is calculated in the display currency

**Example:**
If you have:
- Netflix in USD ($15.99)
- Spotify in EUR (€9.99)
- Disney+ in GBP (£7.99)

You can view the total in JPY, CAD, or any currency you prefer.

<Tip>
  Your currency preference is saved and persists across sessions. It's stored locally in your browser.
</Tip>

### Cloud Sync Across Devices

Your subscription data is automatically synced across all devices in real-time.

**Sync Features:**

- **Real-Time Updates**: Changes appear instantly on all logged-in devices
- **Multi-Device Support**: Access from desktop, mobile, tablet
- **Automatic Backup**: Your data is securely stored in the cloud
- **No Manual Sync Required**: Everything syncs automatically
- **Offline Tolerance**: Local changes sync when you reconnect

**How Cloud Sync Works:**

Subflow uses MongoDB Atlas for cloud storage. When you:
- Add a subscription → Saved to the cloud immediately
- Edit details → Updated across all devices in real-time
- Delete a subscription → Removed everywhere instantly

**Data Stored in the Cloud:**
- All subscription information
- Co-subscriber relationships
- Email notification preferences
- User preferences (amortization settings, etc.)

<Info>
  Currency preference is stored locally in your browser, not in the cloud. This is by design for faster performance.
</Info>

### Email Renewal Notifications

Never miss a subscription renewal with automatic email reminders.

**Notification Features:**

- **Pre-Renewal Reminders**: Emails sent 3 days before subscriptions renew
- **Subscription Details**: Includes service name, price, and payment date
- **Multi-Language Support**: Choose your preferred email language
- **Enable/Disable Toggle**: Turn notifications on or off anytime
- **Custom Email Address**: Use any email (doesn't have to be your login email)

**Setting Up Notifications:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the **More Menu** (three dots) in the toolbar and select **Email Notification**.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enable Notifications">
    Toggle the **Notify** switch to ON.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enter Email Address">
    Provide the email where you want to receive notifications.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Choose Language">
    Select your preferred language for email content (English, Chinese, Japanese, or Spanish).
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Save Settings">
    Click **Save Settings** to activate notifications.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What's Included in Notification Emails:**
- Subscription service name and icon
- Renewal amount and currency
- Payment date
- Payment cycle (monthly/yearly)
- All upcoming subscriptions for the next 3 days

<Note>
  Notifications are sent at 12:00 AM UTC, 3 days before each subscription's renewal date. Make sure your email address is correct and check spam folders if you don't receive them.
</Note>

### Co-Subscriber Management

Share subscriptions with family, friends, or teammates and manage shared costs together.

**Co-Subscriber Features:**

- **Email-Based Invitations**: Invite others by email address
- **Custom Amounts**: Specify how much each co-subscriber pays
- **Multi-Currency**: Each co-subscriber can have their own currency
- **Pending Invitations**: Co-subscribers must accept invitations
- **Status Tracking**: See who has accepted and who is pending
- **Flexible Management**: Add or remove co-subscribers anytime
- **Shared View**: Co-subscribers see the subscription in their calendar

**How Co-Subscribers Work:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit Subscription">
    Open the subscription you want to share and click **Manage Co-Subscribers**.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Add Co-Subscriber">
    Enter the email address of the person you want to invite. They must have a Subflow account.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Set Amount">
    Specify how much this co-subscriber pays. This can be different from the main subscription price.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Choose Currency">
    Select the currency for this co-subscriber's amount.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Send Invitation">
    Click **Add** to send the invitation. The co-subscriber will receive a notification.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Co-Subscriber Accepts">
    The invited user sees a notification (mail icon) in their toolbar. They can accept or reject the invitation.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Shared Subscription">
    Once accepted, the subscription appears in both users' calendars. The co-subscriber sees "Co Subscription" indicator.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Use Cases:**
- Family Netflix or Spotify plans
- Shared team SaaS tools (Notion, Figma, etc.)
- Splitting costs with roommates
- Managing household subscription expenses

**Co-Subscriber Actions:**
- **Owner**: Can edit, delete, add/remove co-subscribers
- **Co-Subscriber**: Can view details and leave the subscription

<Warning>
  Co-subscribers can only be added if they have an active Subflow account with the same email address. The email must match their registered Subflow email exactly.
</Warning>
