Subflow supports multiple languages and currencies, allowing you to track subscriptions in your preferred language and manage costs across different currencies.

## Supported Languages

Subflow is available in four languages:

- **English (en)** - Default
- **Chinese (zh)** - Traditional Chinese
- **Japanese (ja)**
- **Spanish (es)**

## Changing Your Language

Your language preference is automatically stored in a cookie called `subflow-locale` for 30 days.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Language Settings">
    The language is typically set during your first visit or can be changed through your browser settings. Subflow detects your preferred language automatically.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Language Persists Across Sessions">
    Once set, your language preference is saved and will persist across all your devices when you log in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The language setting affects the entire interface, including calendar labels, button text, and notification messages.
</Note>

## Currency Management

### Available Currencies

Subflow supports **all major global currencies** through integration with the ExchangeRate.host API. You can track subscriptions in any currency that matters to you.

### Setting Your Preferred Currency

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Currency Selector">
    Your currency preference is stored in your browser's local storage and syncs across sessions.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Choose Your Currency">
    Select from the comprehensive list of available currencies. The default currency is **USD**.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Currency Persists Locally">
    Your selected currency is saved to `localStorage` and will be remembered on the same device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Multi-Currency Subscriptions

You can add subscriptions in **different currencies**:

- Each subscription can have its own currency
- When adding a subscription, select the currency from the dropdown
- The currency is stored with each subscription record
- Exchange rates are updated every 24 hours for accurate conversion

<Tip>
If you share subscriptions with co-subscribers, each person can split costs in their own currency. See the [Co-Subscribers guide](/guide/co-subscribers) for more details.
</Tip>

## How Currency Data is Managed

### Exchange Rate Updates

- **Currency list**: Refreshed every 7 days
- **Live exchange rates**: Updated every 24 hours
- Exchange rate data comes from ExchangeRate.host API

### Technical Details

The currency system works as follows:

1. **Currency List** (`getCurrenciesList`): Fetches all available currencies with a 7-day cache
2. **Live Rates** (`getCurrenciesLive`): Fetches current exchange rates with a 24-hour cache
3. **Local Storage**: Your preferred currency is stored in `localStorage` under the key `currency`

<Note>
Currency data is cached to improve performance and reduce API calls. The cache is automatically refreshed based on the revalidation period.
</Note>

## Language-Specific Features

### Email Notifications

When you set up email notifications, you can choose the language for your notification emails:

- English
- Chinese (Traditional)
- Japanese
- Spanish

The email content, including subscription reminders and payment notifications, will be sent in your chosen language.

See the [Email Notifications guide](/guide/email-notifications) for setup instructions.

### Date and Number Formatting

Subflow automatically formats dates and numbers according to your selected language:

- Date formats adapt to local conventions
- Number separators (commas, periods) match language standards
- Currency symbols display correctly based on the selected currency

## Best Practices

<Tip>
**For Multi-Currency Users**: If you have subscriptions in multiple currencies, consider setting your preferred display currency to your primary banking currency for easier expense tracking.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**For International Teams**: When sharing subscriptions with co-subscribers in different countries, each person can view costs in their own currency while the original subscription maintains its native currency.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

**Language not changing?**
- Clear your browser cookies and reload the page
- Check that the `subflow-locale` cookie is being set correctly

**Currency not saving?**
- Ensure your browser allows local storage
- Check that you're not in private/incognito mode, which may clear local storage on exit

**Exchange rates seem outdated?**
- Exchange rates are cached for 24 hours for performance
- The cache will automatically refresh after this period
