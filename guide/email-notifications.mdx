Subflow's email notification system sends you reminders before subscription payments are due, helping you stay on top of your recurring expenses.

## How Email Notifications Work

When enabled, Subflow sends you:

- **Payment reminders** before your subscription renewal dates
- **Upcoming payment summaries** showing what you'll be charged for
- **Welcome emails** when you first set up notifications, introducing key features

All emails are sent in your chosen language with beautifully formatted templates.

## Setting Up Email Notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open More Settings Menu">
    On the subscription management page, click the **More Settings** button (three dots icon) in the top navigation.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select Email Notification">
    Choose **Email Notification** from the menu options.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enable Notifications">
    Toggle the **Notify** switch to enable email notifications.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enter Your Email Address">
    Provide the email address where you want to receive notifications. This can be different from your account email.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Choose Email Language">
    Select your preferred language for email notifications:
    - English (en)
    - Chinese (zh)
    - Japanese (ja)
    - Spanish (es)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Save Settings">
    Click **Save Settings** to activate your email notifications.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Your notification email address, language preference, and notification status are securely stored in the database and linked to your user account.
</Note>

## Email Data Model

Your email notification settings are stored with the following information:

- **Email address**: Where notifications are sent
- **Language**: The language for email content (en, zh, ja, or es)
- **Notify status**: Whether notifications are enabled or disabled
- **User ID**: Links settings to your account
- **Timestamps**: When settings were created and last updated

## Email Template Features

Subflow's notification emails include:

### Welcome Email

When you first enable notifications, you'll receive a welcome email featuring:

- **Feature highlights**: Smart Add, Email Notifications, Subscription Analytics, and Co-Subscribers
- **Usage instructions**: Step-by-step guidance for each feature
- **Quick start guide**: Basic operations like adding subscriptions, switching months, and viewing analytics
- **Screenshots**: Visual examples in your selected language

### Payment Reminder Emails

Regular notification emails show:

- Upcoming subscription charges
- Payment dates and amounts
- Subscription names and services
- Total monthly spend summary

## Managing Your Email Settings

### Updating Your Email Address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Return to Email Notification Settings">
    Click **More Settings** → **Email Notification**.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Update Email Field">
    Enter your new email address in the email input field.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click **Save Settings** to update your notification email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Changing Email Language

You can change the language of your notification emails at any time:

1. Open **Email Notification** settings
2. Select a different language from the **Email Language** dropdown
3. Click **Save Settings**

All future emails will be sent in the newly selected language.

### Disabling Notifications

To stop receiving email notifications:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Email Settings">
    Navigate to **More Settings** → **Email Notification**.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Turn Off Notify">
    Toggle the **Notify** switch to the off position.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Settings** to disable notifications.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Your email address and language preferences are preserved even when notifications are disabled, so you can easily re-enable them later without re-entering your information.
</Tip>

## Email Validation

Subflow validates your email address to ensure notifications are delivered successfully:

- Email must be in a valid format (e.g., `user@example.com`)
- Invalid email addresses will show an error message
- You must provide an email address when notifications are enabled

## Technical Details

### Database Storage

Email settings are stored in the `emails` collection with:

- **Unique constraint**: One email setting per user (indexed on `userId`)
- **Required fields**: `userId`, `language`, and `notify` status
- **Optional field**: `email` (required only when notifications are enabled)
- **Automatic timestamps**: Created and updated times are tracked

### Upsert Functionality

The email settings system uses an "upsert" operation:

- If settings exist for your account, they are **updated**
- If no settings exist, they are **created**
- This ensures seamless updates without duplicate records

Implemented in `upsertEmail` function at `app/actions/action.ts:327`.

## Troubleshooting

**Not receiving emails?**
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Verify your email address is correct in settings
- Ensure the **Notify** toggle is enabled
- Confirm your email provider isn't blocking Subflow emails

**Emails in wrong language?**
- Open Email Notification settings and verify the **Email Language** selection
- Save settings again to ensure the change is applied

**Settings not saving?**
- Check your internet connection
- Try logging out and logging back in
- Ensure you have proper authentication permissions
