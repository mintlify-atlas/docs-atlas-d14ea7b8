Co-subscribers let you share your subscriptions with family and friends, making it easy to split costs and manage shared subscription expenses together.

## What are Co-Subscribers?

The co-subscriber feature allows you to:

- **Invite others** to share a subscription with you
- **Split costs** by assigning specific amounts to each person
- **Track shared subscriptions** separately from your personal ones
- **Manage invitations** through a confirmation system

## How Co-Subscribers Work

### The Invitation Flow

1. **Owner adds co-subscribers**: You add someone's email to a subscription with their share amount
2. **System validates**: Subflow checks if the email is registered
3. **Invitation sent**: The co-subscriber receives an invitation notification
4. **Confirmation required**: The invited person must accept or reject the invitation
5. **Access granted**: Once accepted, the subscription appears in their subscription list

### Co-Subscriber Status

Each co-subscriber has a status:

- **Pending** (`confirm: false`): Invitation sent but not yet accepted
- **Accepted** (`confirm: true`): Invitation accepted, sharing active

## Adding Co-Subscribers to a Subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a Subscription">
    Click on an existing subscription from your calendar or subscription list to edit it.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Navigate to Co-Subscribers Tab">
    In the subscription dialog, go to the **Co-Subscribers** section.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enter Amount and Currency">
    - Enter the **amount** the co-subscriber will pay
    - Select their **currency** from the dropdown
    - Each co-subscriber can have a different amount and currency
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enter Email Address">
    Type the co-subscriber's email address. This must be an email registered with Subflow.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Add Co-Subscriber">
    Click the **Add** button to send the invitation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The email address must belong to a registered Subflow user. If the email is not found, you'll see an error message prompting them to register first.
</Note>

### Co-Subscriber Validation

When adding a co-subscriber, Subflow validates:

- **Email format**: Must be a valid email address
- **Registration status**: Email must be registered in Subflow
- **Uniqueness**: Can't add the same email twice
- **Self-invitation**: You cannot add your own email
- **Amount**: Must be greater than 0

## Managing Your Co-Subscribers

### Viewing Co-Subscribers

Co-subscribers are listed under each subscription with:

- Email address
- Assigned amount and currency
- Status (Pending or Accepted)

### Removing a Co-Subscriber

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subscription">
    Click on the subscription containing the co-subscriber you want to remove.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Find the Co-Subscriber">
    In the **Co-Subscribers** list, locate the person you want to remove.
  </Step>

## Multi-Currency Cost Splitting

One powerful feature of co-subscribers is **multi-currency support**:

- Each co-subscriber can have their own currency
- The subscription owner sets the amount each person pays
- Exchange rates are handled automatically
- Everyone sees costs in their preferred currency

### Example Scenario

- **Subscription**: Netflix Premium - $15.99/month (USD)
- **Co-subscriber 1**: Pays €5.00 (EUR)
- **Co-subscriber 2**: Pays ¥800 (JPY)
- **Owner**: Pays the remaining amount in USD

Each person tracks their portion in their own currency.

## Security and Privacy

### Email Verification

For security, Subflow:

- Validates that invitation emails match the authenticated user
- Checks email registration before allowing invitations
- Prevents unauthorized access to shared subscriptions

Implemented in `app/actions/action.ts:139` and `app/actions/action.ts:176`.

### Access Control

Co-subscribers can:

- **View** the subscription in their list
- **Track** their portion of the cost
- **Leave** the subscription at any time

Co-subscribers **cannot**:

- Edit the subscription details
- Add or remove other co-subscribers
- Delete the original subscription

Only the subscription owner has full edit and delete permissions.

## Best Practices

<Tip>

</Tip>

</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

**Can't add a co-subscriber?**
- Verify the email address is correct
- Confirm the person has registered for Subflow
- Check that you haven't already added this email
- Ensure the email isn't your own

**Co-subscriber not seeing the invitation?**
- Ask them to check the mail icon in their navigation
- Confirm they're logged into the correct Subflow account
- Verify their email matches the one you entered

**Invitation not showing as accepted?**
- The co-subscriber must click **Confirm** on the invitation
- Check internet connection during confirmation
- Try refreshing the subscription page

**Can't remove a co-subscriber?**
- Ensure you're the subscription owner (not a co-subscriber yourself)
- Try editing the subscription first, then removing the co-subscriber
- Check that you have a stable connection when saving changes
